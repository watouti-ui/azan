    About

    Times come from the timetable published by the mosque — nothing is calculated or fetched. Accuracy is the accuracy of that table.

    Background limits: a web app cannot schedule alarms while it is closed. The Azan plays and notifications fire only while this app is open. For a guaranteed alarm with the screen off, use your phone's clock app alongside this one.
