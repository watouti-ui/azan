    About

    Times come from the timetable you import — nothing is calculated or fetched. Accuracy is the accuracy of your table.

    Background limits: a web app cannot schedule alarms while it is closed. The Azan plays and notifications fire only while this app is open (foreground, or a recent background tab on Android). For a guaranteed alarm with the screen off, use your phone's clock/alarm app alongside this one.
